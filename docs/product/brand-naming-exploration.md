# Cadence Brand Naming Exploration

Last reviewed: August 3, 2026

## Decision for this release

Keep **Cadence** in the product for now. A rename touches OAuth branding,
domains, email identity, legal documents, billing, analytics, screenshots,
support material, and customer trust. Do not rename from a brainstorm alone.

The names below are creative candidates, not cleared names. No trademark,
company-name, social-handle, app-store, or domain availability is claimed.
Before adopting any candidate, run a professional trademark search in every
launch market, check relevant software and communications classes, review
common-law use, secure domains and handles, and obtain counsel approval.

## Naming criteria

A strong name should be easy to say, easy to spell after hearing it once,
credible for sales teams and agencies, broad enough for future workflows, and
distinct from generic email-delivery or high-volume spam tooling. It should
support the position: responsible Gmail outreach that creates qualified
conversations through thoughtful automation and useful AI assistance.

## Recommended shortlist

1. **Cadence** for continuity and product fit, subject to clearance.
2. **Threadwell** for a human, conversation-first promise.
3. **Replylane** for the clearest outcome story.
4. **Signalcraft** for a premium, expandable platform position.
5. **Mailstride** for measured progress and sending discipline.

## Next naming gate

Choose three finalists, then complete legal clearance, domain and handle
checks, customer pronunciation tests, category-fit interviews, and a migration
cost estimate. Only then should the product or repository be renamed.
